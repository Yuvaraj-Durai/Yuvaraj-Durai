### 🔬 About Me

I'm a PhD researcher working at the intersection of **biomedical signal processing** and **embedded intelligence**.
My doctoral research focuses on **ECG signal analysis and bioinformatics**, and I bring that into real hardware
through **embedded systems, IoT devices, and semiconductor-level design** — with **Machine Learning and Deep
Learning** as the thread that connects the signal to the silicon.

- 🧠 **Research focus:** ECG/biosignal analysis, bioinformatics, physiological signal modeling
- ⚙️ **Systems focus:** Embedded systems, IoT edge devices, semiconductor/hardware design
- 🤖 **AI focus:** Machine Learning & Deep Learning — especially on-device/edge inference for biomedical signals
- 🎯 **Goal:** Bridging the gap between algorithm research and deployable, efficient hardware
